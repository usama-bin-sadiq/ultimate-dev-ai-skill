# CONTEXT

## Glossary

**order**: a customer's intent to buy one or more items, tracked from placement through settlement.
**order item**: a single line on an order, holding a product and a quantity.
**customer**: a person or organisation that places orders.
**invoice**: the document a customer is asked to pay against one or more orders.
**fulfilment**: the work of picking, packing, and dispatching an order.
**LedgerEntry**: a row in the double-entry ledger.
**subscription**: a recurring agreement to be invoiced on a schedule.
