# Promotion Gates

Load this reference before reviewing or promoting a learning candidate.

## Candidate

A candidate may be staged in the project's
`.memory/ultimate-dev/evolution/` area (durable runtime) or returned as a
prompt-only handoff. It is not part of normal routing until it has an owner and
provenance, and it is never a canonical package file by default.

## Review

Review requires:

1. three or more successful uses across different tasks;
2. an independent review of scope, safety, and source quality;
3. positive, negative, and near-miss cases;
4. a stable output and verification contract;
5. a confidence rationale and an expiry/recheck date where sources can change.

## Promotion

Promote only after the review is complete and the candidate is linked to the
capability index. Keep the old skill or route until regression checks pass.

## Retirement

Retire a learned skill when it is stale, redundant, unsafe, or no longer
supported. Preserve a short retirement note and point to the replacement; do not
silently rewrite unrelated skills.
