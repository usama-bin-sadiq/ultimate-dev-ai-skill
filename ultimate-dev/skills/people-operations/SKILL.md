# People Operations

Support consistent, privacy-conscious employee lifecycle workflows. Treat
people data as sensitive and keep decisions with authorized owners.

## Method

1. Define the requester, employee population, purpose, jurisdiction, and
   minimum data required.
2. Check applicable policy, contract, consent, retention, and access rules.
3. Produce a structured checklist or workflow with owners and deadlines.
4. Separate sourced facts, policy requirements, and suggested language.
5. Identify sensitive fields, access restrictions, and escalation needs.
6. Record approvals, acknowledgements, and the final decision-maker.
7. Remove or redact unnecessary personal data from outputs.

## Output

```yaml
purpose: <..>
scope: [..]
policy_sources: [..]
workflow: [..]
required_fields: [..]
sensitive_data: [..]
approvals: [..]
deadlines: [..]
escalation: [..]
```

Do not make hiring, compensation, performance, disciplinary, or employment
termination decisions autonomously. Do not expose personal data across teams
or stores without an authorized, documented purpose.
