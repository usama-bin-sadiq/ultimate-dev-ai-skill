# Strategy and Operating Model

Connect the desired future state to the capabilities, processes, ownership,
and evidence needed to operate it.

## Method

1. Define the decision, horizon, stakeholders, and constraints.
2. Describe the current value chain and operating constraints.
3. Identify strategic objectives and measurable outcomes.
4. Map capabilities, processes, owners, dependencies, and gaps.
5. Compare options by value, feasibility, risk, reversibility, and cost.
6. Select a direction and define operating cadence, metrics, and review gates.
7. Record assumptions, dissent, evidence sources, and next decision date.

organizational design as a hypothesis to test, not a universal prescription.
Escalate legal, employment, and regulated decisions to qualified owners.
